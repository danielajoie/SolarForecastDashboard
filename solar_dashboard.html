Calibration & CV days

Non-CV days with weather GHI: ratio = actual / modeled, recency-weighted (latest 3 free days weighted higher). Result multiplies the 7-day forecast and CV-day potential estimates.

CV = Y: SOC ≳90%, MPPTs floated. Recorded kWh is real but capacity-limited (lower bound). Excluded from calibration. Potential = model × correction; forgone = max(0, potential − actual).
