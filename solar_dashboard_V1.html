Orientation notes

EAST_DERATE (0.90): East azimuth, shallow 15° tilt — strong morning production, earlier roll-off in afternoon. Slightly below an ideal south-facing reference.

WEST_DERATE (0.93): West azimuth, 35° tilt — better summer incidence at mid-latitudes and captures afternoon sun; modest derate vs due-south optimum.

Both multipliers are relative to a simple horizontal-GHI × nameplate model and are refined further by the learned correction factor from your CSV.

Calibration & CV days

For each non-CV day with both actual kWh and weather GHI, compute ratio = actual_total / modeled_total. Apply a recency-weighted average: the most recent 3 non-CV days get weight 3, 2, 1.5 respectively; older days get weight 1. Result multiplies the 7-day forecast.

CV = Y means SOC ≳90% and MPPTs floated: recorded kWh is real but capacity-limited (a lower bound). Those days are excluded from calibration so the correction factor reflects true array capability. For CV days with weather GHI, the dashboard shows model “potential” (weather × efficiency × derates × correction) and estimated forgone kWh = max(0, potential − actual).
